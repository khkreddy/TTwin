# 90 · Three-state diagnosis

Non-negotiable spine (chapter and curriculum intelligence definition):

- **Mastery** — holds the correct operative model (unassisted, later confirmed).
- **Misconception (Mx)** — holds a **named** wrong model: a corruption of `mechanism_core` with a canonical wrong output.
- **Lack of Knowledge (LoK)** — no operative model (guessing, everyday language).

Absence of knowledge is **not** a misconception. A scalar “ability” is a defect. A hinge is a cognitive obligation, not a section heading. Mx count is state-proportionate, never a quota. Zero admitted mx on a hinge is a census outcome, not a gap to fill.

## Rules

- Mx requires a stored CANDIDATE (or later VALIDATED) row on **that** hinge. Do not invent a type.
- Chemistry: at most one mx name in student feedback, matched to the chosen distractor, badged unverified, and not printed as a ledger.
- Phy/bio: mx empty on the interim map → Mastery / LoK / unclassified error only.
- Anti-narration: probes and pre-Finish UI must not donate the criterion (“new substance is formed”).
- Frequency claims (“most students reverse K”) are banned. Generator counts are not classroom frequencies.

## Corruption types (map vocabulary, teacher-side)

`term_substitution` · `condition_omission` · `relationship_reversal` · `scope_error` · `surface_feature_capture` · `mechanism_conflation` · `operation_confusion`

Count is state-proportionate, never a quota. A type marked not-applicable stays not-applicable.

## Diagnosis is a lookup where the metadata exists

A chosen wrong option with a mapped misconception names the trap. No model call, full audit trail. Where the metadata does not exist, the state is LoK or unclassified — not a synthesized mx.
