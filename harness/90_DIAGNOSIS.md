# 90 · Three-state diagnosis

Non-negotiable spine (`TeacherTwin_ChapterAndCurriculum_Intelligence_Definition`):

- **Mastery** — holds the correct operative model (unassisted, later confirmed).
- **Misconception (Mx)** — holds a **named** wrong model: a corruption of `mechanism_core` with a canonical wrong output.
- **Lack of Knowledge (LoK)** — no operative model (guessing, everyday language).

Absence of knowledge is **not** a misconception. A scalar “ability” is a defect.

## Rules

- Mx requires a stored CANDIDATE (or later VALIDATED) row on **that** hinge. Do not invent a type.
- Chemistry: at most one mx name in student feedback, matched to the chosen distractor, badged unverified.
- Phy/bio: mx empty on the interim map → Mastery / LoK / unclassified error only.
- Anti-narration: probes and pre-Finish UI must not donate the criterion (“new substance is formed”).
- Frequency claims (“most students reverse K”) are banned. Generator counts are not classroom frequencies.
