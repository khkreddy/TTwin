# 95 · Strings

## Allowed (public UI and student copy)

- AI
- UNCALIBRATED
- CANDIDATE
- generated analysis — not a published mark scheme
- Mix-ups shown to teachers are unverified
- This map is a chapter list until a complete hinge map exists (phy/bio)
- This feedback was drafted by AI and checked against available notes. It is not a mark scheme.
- N of M items had no verified key and were not scored.
- This is one paper on one day. It is evidence, not a measure of you.
- Detailed topic wiring for this subject is in preparation.
- Guided practice modes are not available in this build.
- Learner accounts are not enabled in this build.
- Additional mathematics items are in preparation.
- Open-response physics practice is in preparation.
- AI could not refine
- AI feedback unavailable
- No verified notes for this item

## Banned (public TTwin)

- Vendor names, model slugs, provider product names (lint: never render them in UI, footers, errors, or student copy)
- “most students”, “common error”, classroom frequency claims
- “you learned from this mistake”
- “keep practicing” as a next step (name the act)
- SMILES on the paper (draw, do not print)
- crop paths, examiner comments, mx ledgers on the learner sheet
- official mark scheme (unless a future freeze actually ships one)
- undifferentiated LEARNED
- presenting a chapter list as a hinge map
- implying a dark layer is about to ship

Internal review apps and this lint list may name what they ban. GitHub Pages display copy must not.
